CookieGhost — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 26, 2026

Summary

CookieGhost does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All processing happens locally in your browser. No data ever leaves your device.

What CookieGhost Does

CookieGhost replaces tracking cookie values with randomly generated data directly in your browser. It intercepts cookies set by known tracking services and replaces their values with realistic but meaningless random strings. Functional cookies (login sessions, CSRF tokens, preferences) are never modified.

Data Collection

We collect no data whatsoever. Specifically:

  • No browsing history is collected or transmitted
  • No cookie values (original or randomized) are sent anywhere
  • No personal information is gathered
  • No analytics or telemetry are used
  • No user accounts are required
  • No network requests are made by the extension itself

Local Storage

CookieGhost stores the following data locally in your browser using the Chrome Storage API:

  • Extension settings — your chosen protection mode, whitelisted/blacklisted sites
  • Session seeds — temporary random values used to maintain session consistency (cleared on browser restart)
  • Aggregate statistics — counts of how many cookies were randomized/preserved (no cookie names or values are stored)

This data never leaves your browser and can be cleared at any time from the extension settings.

Permissions

CookieGhost requests the following permissions, each strictly necessary for its core functionality:

  • cookies — to detect and modify tracking cookies
  • storage — to save your settings locally
  • activeTab — to show per-site statistics in the popup
  • alarms — to schedule periodic seed resets
  • declarativeNetRequest — to block cookies from known tracker domains
  • host_permissions (<all_urls>) — required to intercept tracking cookies on any website you visit

Third-Party Services

CookieGhost uses no third-party services, SDKs, analytics, or external APIs.

Open Source

CookieGhost's source code is fully available for inspection. You can verify that no data collection occurs.

Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this privacy policy will be reflected in the extension update notes and this document.
