====================================
CHROME WEB STORE LISTING — CookieGhost
====================================

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 132 chars):
Anonymize tracking cookies with realistic random data. Websites work normally — but trackers get garbage.

CATEGORY: Productivity (or Privacy & Security if available)

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
---
CookieGhost doesn't block cookies. It doesn't delete them. It poisons them.

Every website you visit drops tracking cookies to build a profile about you — your browsing habits, interests, purchases, and identity. Traditional privacy tools block or delete these cookies, which often breaks websites or triggers annoying cookie walls.

CookieGhost takes a different approach: it lets tracking cookies exist, but replaces their values with realistic random data in real-time. Google Analytics gets a fake client ID. Facebook gets a bogus pixel ID. Trackers think they're working, but your profile is pure noise.

HOW IT WORKS:
- Automatically detects 60+ known tracking cookies (Google Analytics, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, HubSpot, and more)
- Replaces values with format-correct random data that passes validation
- Preserves functional cookies (login sessions, CSRF tokens, preferences) so websites work normally
- Strips cookies entirely from 50+ hardcore tracker domains

THREE PROTECTION MODES:
- Aggressive: Randomizes all uncertain cookies
- Balanced: Smart classification with conservative defaults
- Gentle: Only randomizes confirmed tracking cookies

PRIVACY FIRST:
- Zero data collection — everything stays on your device
- No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry
- Open source and fully auditable

Don't block. Don't delete. Poison the well.
---

SINGLE PURPOSE DESCRIPTION (required by Chrome):
CookieGhost replaces tracking cookie values with random data so websites work normally but ad trackers receive meaningless information.

PERMISSIONS JUSTIFICATION:
- cookies: Core functionality — detecting and modifying tracking cookies in real-time
- storage: Saving user settings (protection mode, whitelisted sites) locally
- activeTab: Displaying per-site cookie statistics in the extension popup
- alarms: Scheduling periodic randomization seed resets
- declarativeNetRequest + declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess: Blocking cookies from known tracker domains via header modification rules
- host_permissions <all_urls>: Required to intercept tracking cookies on any website the user visits. The extension must monitor cookie changes across all sites to provide comprehensive tracking protection.
