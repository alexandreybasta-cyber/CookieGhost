# CookieGhost — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: March 2026**

## What CookieGhost Does
CookieGhost replaces tracking cookie values with random data in real-time. It does not block or delete cookies — websites continue to function normally.

## Data We Collect
**CookieGhost collects NO personal data.** Specifically:
- We do not collect your browsing history
- We do not read page content
- We do not transmit any data to external servers
- We do not store cookie values (only anonymized counts)

## Data Stored Locally
CookieGhost stores the following **only on your device**:
- Your settings (protection mode, whitelist/blacklist)
- Per-session random seeds (reset when browser closes)
- Anonymized statistics (cookie counts only — no URLs, no values)

## Third Parties
CookieGhost does not share any data with third parties. Ever.
